How To Shape a Beret

Video Tutorial

Quick Steps

1. Shave the beret inside and out with a disposable razor until smooth. 2. Soak in warm (not hot) water. 3. Place on your head, center the flash over your left eye. 4. Fold excess material toward your right ear. 5. Let it dry on your head or a mannequin. 6. Repeat as needed until it holds its shape.

The JROTC black necktie is worn with a four-in-hand or modified Windsor knot. The front of the tie must be longer than the rear, pulled up to cover the collar button of the blouse/shirt. Tie clasps are not authorized.

The Male Uniform

Video Tutorial · Proper Wear Guide

🔍 Search YouTube: JROTC Male Uniform Guide
✏ Admin: Paste a specific YouTube embed URL above once you pick a video

The Class A male uniform includes the Army blue coat, trousers, gray long or short sleeve shirt, black necktie, beret, black web belt with brass buckle, black dress shoes, and black socks. All brass must be polished. Ribbons, badges, and insignia must be properly placed.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

✏ Click below to edit
What is Army JROTC? +
Army JROTC is a leadership education program that teaches high school students citizenship, service, and personal responsibility through military science and physical training.
How do I join the program? +
Enrollment is open to any student in the school. Simply speak with the JROTC instructor or visit the main office to fill out enrollment paperwork.
What do cadets wear? +
Cadets are required to wear their Class A or B uniform on designated uniform days. Uniforms are provided by the program. See the Video Tutorials section for wear guidance.
Does JROTC mean I have to join the military? +
No. JROTC does not obligate a cadet to serve in the military. It is a leadership and character development program for all students regardless of career goals.
What competitions are available? +
We offer Drill Team, Color Guard, Raider Team, Academic Bowl, and Marksmanship competitions throughout the school year.

Description of the Course

About the JROTC curriculum

✏ Click below to edit

The Army Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps (JROTC) is a leadership education program. Students earn elective credit while developing essential life skills.

Curriculum Overview

The course covers leadership theory, military history, citizenship responsibilities, first aid, physical fitness, and map reading/navigation.

Grading

Students are evaluated on class participation, uniform wear, physical fitness assessments, written exams, and participation in scheduled activities and events.

Credit

JROTC fulfills the Physical Education requirement and counts as an elective credit toward graduation. Check with your counselor for specific credit policies.

JROTC History & Creed

✏ Click below to edit

History of JROTC

The National Defense Act of 1916 established ROTC programs in colleges and high schools. Today, Army JROTC serves over 275,000 cadets in approximately 1,700 high schools across the United States and overseas.

The program has grown to become one of the largest and most successful youth leadership programs in the country, producing generations of leaders in every field of American life.

The JROTC Creed

I am an Army Junior ROTC Cadet. I will always conduct myself to bring credit to my family, country, school and the Corps of Cadets. I am loyal and patriotic. I am the future of the United States of America. I do not lie, cheat or steal and will always be accountable for my actions and deeds. I will always practice good citizenship and patriotism. I will work hard to improve my mind and strengthen my body. I will seek the mantle of leadership and stand prepared to uphold the Constitution and the American way of life. May God grant me the strength to always live by this creed.
